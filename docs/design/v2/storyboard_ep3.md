### `walls` · 2:51–4:15（83.7 秒，配乐 `tension`）

**画面**：Clawd 一路奔跑，依次撞上四堵墙：算力墙、品味墙、并行墙、现实墙。并行墙前，它复制出一万个自己一起冲（“考考你”：快多少倍？）。每堵墙都有标牌和来源小字；撞上、绕过、再撞下一堵。

- `2:52` **旁白**：怀疑派还说：这条路上，有好几堵墙。
  <br><sub>EN: The skeptics add: there are walls on this road.</sub>
- `2:56` **Clawd**：墙？加速派说 r 大于 1——那我就全速冲过去！
  <br><sub>EN: Walls? The fast camp says r is above 1 — so I’ll charge right through!</sub>
- `3:01` **旁白**：算力墙：好点子要靠实验验证，实验要靠芯片来算——这就是“算力”。一次大训练要跑好几个月：想得再快，也得等它跑完。
  <br><sub>EN: The compute wall: ideas are tested by experiments, and experiments run on chips — that’s “compute”. A big training run takes months: however fast you think, you wait for it to finish.</sub>
- `3:12` **旁白**：品味墙：普林斯顿的研究者，用“还没发表的论文”里的题目考 AI——它不可能提前见过答案。这叫“影子评测”。
  <br><sub>EN: The taste wall: Princeton researchers tested AI on questions from papers not yet published — so it couldn’t have seen the answers. A “shadow evaluation”.</sub>
- `3:20` **旁白**：结果：AI 很会动手做实验，却不太会判断“该研究什么”——探索得太少，结论下得太快。

  <br><sub>EN: One of me isn’t fast enough? Then copy me ten thousand times and charge together!</sub>

  <br><sub>EN: The answer: about 100×. That’s the parallel wall: a rule of thumb says speed grows only with the square root of the number — four times as many, only twice as fast.</sub>

  <br><sub>EN: Because they often think alike: ten thousand of me, all having the same idea.</sub>
